# Tavvy cruise catalog — research and implementation in progress

## Approved scope

All identifiable operating overnight ocean, river and expedition cruise ships worldwide. Exclude private charter-only yachts and sightseeing/day-trip vessels. A public, individually bookable overnight yacht cruise may qualify; its marketing name alone does not determine exclusion. Record announced vessels separately, never as currently operating. Research began20September2026.

## Customer experience

A ship is a Tavvy Universe containing verified dining, entertainment, retail, recreation, spa and other venues. A ship review refers to that ship and a sailing date; venue reviews remain attached to the venue. Ports and shore excursions are separate places linked to a sailing, never mistaken for the ship. Cabin categories can be reviewed without requiring travelers to disclose a cabin number or current whereabouts.

Keep reviews prominent. Use Overview, On board, Cabins, Photos & Stories and Ship information where content exists. Reuse the existing tap/review system and keep the four-summary grid. The ship’s main experience covers onboard comfort, food, entertainment and upkeep. Specialist venue taps follow the venue type.

## Fact definitions

Store each fact with its source URL, date checked, units/basis and verification state. Unknown is null; conflicting reports remain identified until reconciled.

- Identity: ship name, aliases, operator, vessel type, IMO when officially available, river ENI where applicable. Internal stable identity must survive rename/operator changes.
- Dates: year built, entry into service and major refurbishment dates are separate.
- Size: length in meters; gross tonnage as a volume measure, never ship weight.
- Capacity: double-occupancy/lower-berth capacity and maximum guest capacity are separate; record crew separately.
- Decks: total decks and passenger-accessible decks are separate.
- Dining: restaurants/dining outlets, cafes and bars are separate categories; label source counting method. Listed venues do not prove complete totals.
- Shops, pools, accessibility features, cabin categories and deck-plan links require ship-specific evidence.
- Shows and venue availability may vary by sailing/date. Do not promise a specific performance on every trip.

Do not fabricate reviews, endorsements, occupancy, operating status, venue counts, deck coordinates or ship photos. Ship/venue identity and operating status are publication gates; unverified optional facts stay absent. No borrowed marketing descriptions or copied deck-plan artwork in the product; link official resources unless permitted assets are available.

## Research process and completeness

1. Maintain an operator/source registry across regions, not just CLIA members.
2. Discover ship candidates from official fleet indexes; directory presence alone is not verified current operation.
3. Verify individual identity/status and core facts using operator pages, official fact sheets and registries where needed.
4. Reconcile name changes, overlapping brands and charter arrangements before deduplication.
5. Track per-operator candidate counts, verified ships, missing core facts and inaccessible sources. Do not claim worldwide completion from a subset or a trade association membership count.
6. Publish idempotently after review and retain provenance and change history.

The research files are not a production seed and have not been deployed. Source capture helpers only read public pages; unavailable pages must remain recorded rather than silently omitted or retried through access-control workarounds.
